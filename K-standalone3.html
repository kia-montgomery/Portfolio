The Problem

The organization's onboarding process spanned 14 disconnected systems, required an average of 22 business days to complete, and produced a 31% first-year attrition rate. HR staff spent 8 hours per new hire on manual data entry. New hire NPS was 42 — below the industry benchmark of 58. The cost of doing nothing: $2.5M+ per year in ongoing attrition and inefficiency.

"The platform will leverage natural language processing to auto-populate forms, machine learning to surface role-specific learning paths, and predictive analytics to flag at-risk new hires within the first 90 days." — Project Charter, Section 1

Definition of Ready Checklist

✓ A story is READY when:
  • Written as: "As a [user], I want [action] so that [outcome]"
  • Acceptance criteria are specific and testable
  • Story is small enough to complete in one sprint
  • Dependencies are identified and resolved or planned
  • Team has enough context to begin without clarification
  • Story points estimated with team consensus
