What This Artifact Demonstrates

Effective stakeholder management is one of the highest-weighted domains in the PMP exam and one of the most critical real-world PM skills. This artifact shows my ability to identify, segment, and engage stakeholders strategically — ensuring the right people receive the right information through the right channel at the right cadence.

Beyond communication logistics, this document defines accountability through a RACI matrix and prevents confusion during escalation through a clearly tiered decision path. These are the artifacts that prevent miscommunication, missed decisions, and scope creep on large cross-functional programs.

Design principle: communication cadence was deliberately tiered to protect executive time while keeping the delivery team moving fast. The CHRO sees monthly summaries. The project team sees daily standups. The middle tier — bi-weekly steering reviews — is where decisions get made.
