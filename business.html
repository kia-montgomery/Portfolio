What This Artifact Demonstrates

Business cases are the bridge between a business problem and a funding decision. They require financial modeling, alternatives analysis, and the ability to speak to both operational leaders (who care about process impact) and financial leaders (who care about ROI and payback). This is one of the most powerful artifacts in a PM portfolio — it shows strategic thinking, not just execution.

This case was built to justify a $1.25M budget request and secure CHRO and CFO approval. Every financial figure is grounded in a specific data source: HR analytics, industry benchmarks, or time studies conducted during Phase 1 discovery.

The Problem Statement

Based on process mapping conducted in January 2025, the organization's onboarding process suffers from five compounding failures: 14 disconnected systems with no integration requiring HR staff to re-enter data multiple times per hire; 22-day average time-to-productivity (3× the 7-day industry benchmark); 31% first-year attrition costing ~$8,200 per departure; 8 hours of HR staff time per new hire on automatable administrative tasks; and a new hire NPS of 42 — below the industry average of 58.
