Future State Process Flow

The redesigned process eliminates 18 of 22 manual handoff points through intelligent automation, AI personalization, and integrated system workflows.

FULL AUTO
Stage 1 · Offer Acceptance Trigger
Candidate accepts offer → instant cascade
AI Platform auto-creates onboarding profile. IT ticket auto-generated in ServiceNow. Welcome email sent. All triggered simultaneously from Workday. No human action required between offer acceptance and Day 1 preparation. Systems: Workday + AI Platform + ServiceNow + Email.
HIGH AUTO
Stage 2 · Pre-Boarding (Days 1–3)
Personalized journey before Day 1
New hire completes digital paperwork via DocuSign (pre-populated from Workday data). AI generates a personalized 30-60-90 day plan based on role. Role-specific learning path assigned in LMS. New hire guided — not administered. Systems: AI Platform + DocuSign + LMS.
HIGH AUTO
Stage 3 · Day 1
Equipment ready. Credentials live. Manager intro on calendar.
IT equipment delivered and credentials provisioned before first login. Manager intro meeting auto-scheduled. First learning module auto-launched. Zero waiting. Systems: ServiceNow + Active Directory + Calendar + LMS.
HIGH AUTO
Stage 4 · 30-Day Milestone
AI-driven sentiment and attrition scoring
AI analyzes new hire chatbot interactions via NLP sentiment analysis. Predictive attrition score generated. HR manager automatically notified if employee is flagged as at-risk. Humans retain all final decisions — AI surfaces the signal. Systems: AI Platform + HR Dashboard.
MED AUTO
Stage 5 · 60 & 90-Day Reviews
Structured check-ins with AI-informed coaching
Survey auto-sent at 60 and 90 days. Manager receives AI-generated coaching nudges based on sentiment trends. Attrition risk score updated. Completion progress tracked in real-time dashboard. Systems: AI Platform + Survey Tool + HR Dashboard.

Design Principles Applied

  • Automation First: Every manual step was evaluated for automation; only human judgment and relationship-building steps remain manual.
  • Single Source of Truth: Workday HRIS is the master record; all other systems pull from it via real-time API integration.
  • AI Augmentation: AI tools surface insights and recommendations to HR managers — humans retain all final decisions.
  • Compliance by Design: Privacy, data minimization, and access controls are embedded in the workflow, not bolted on.
  • Continuous Improvement: NPS scores and attrition predictions feed a model retraining loop reviewed quarterly.
