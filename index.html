About Me

Originally from Houston, I attended Howard University in Washington, D.C. — where I earned my MBA and was introduced to consulting. I started my career in HR, spending years across operations, recruiting, workforce planning, and enterprise software implementation.

My work grew into project management, where I became responsible for coordinating large teams, managing deliverables, and keeping complex programs moving. I've always been drawn to the part of a job where I get to figure something out — especially when there isn't already a perfect process in place.

Moving into full-time consulting, I realized my HR background wasn't a detour — it was a differentiator. I understood what it meant to be on the other side of an implementation, which made me a better PM.

A lot of my recent learning has focused on emerging AI and the rapid changes happening in technology. I approach this deliberately — not just following what's popular, but understanding how AI applies to real delivery work.

I've built hands-on experience with Microsoft Copilot, Claude, IBM WatsonX, and ChatGPT. At IBM, I founded and led a generative AI working group that produced 15+ use cases and a WatsonX assistant demo that informed leadership's cost-benefit decisions on AI adoption.

I see AI as a capability multiplier for project managers — and I want to be the kind of PM who already knows how to use it when it becomes expected.

Outside of work, I love traveling with my husband, trying new restaurants, working out, and spending time with family and friends. I volunteer with SEARCH Homeless Services in Houston.

One of my long-term personal goals is to build a family farm in Natchez, Mississippi — a nod to my family roots and the kind of long-term, intentional project I find meaningful outside of work.

My career has taken turns I couldn't have planned when I started in HR, and I'm glad it did. I enjoy building on what I know, learning what I don't, and staying open to where that takes me next.
