What This Artifact Demonstrates

Executive status reports are one of the most critical PM communication skills — and the hardest to get right. Written too long, executives stop reading. Written too short, they miss the nuance. The goal is to get a senior leader with 8 minutes to read it to a) understand exactly where the project stands, b) see what requires their attention, and c) make or defer any decisions clearly.

This report demonstrates my ability to synthesize complex project data into an executive-ready format: RAG status, KPIs, accomplishments, forward actions, and decision requests — all calibrated to the CHRO's level of interest and authority.
