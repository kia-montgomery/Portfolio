What This Artifact Demonstrates

A project schedule is the PM's primary tool for controlling delivery. This Gantt chart demonstrates my ability to decompose a 9-month initiative into 21 sequenced tasks across 4 phases, with named owners, realistic durations, and live progress tracking. It's the artifact that turns a high-level plan into an actionable delivery commitment.

This schedule was also used to manage two change requests (CR-001: ServiceNow addition; CR-004: 2-week Phase 2 extension) — demonstrating that I treat the schedule as a living document that absorbs change through formal process, not informal workarounds.
