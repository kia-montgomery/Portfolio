What This Artifact Demonstrates

Meeting minutes are often overlooked in PM portfolios — but they reveal a critical skill: the ability to run structured, decision-focused meetings and translate discussion into accountable action. Two artifacts are presented here deliberately: the kickoff Steering Committee meeting represents formal program governance, while the Sprint 6 review represents agile cadence — showing fluency across both delivery frameworks.

Notice what's in every set of minutes: a clear agenda, structured discussion notes, explicit decisions with a named decision-maker, and action items with owners and hard deadlines. This is what separates a PM who runs meetings from one who facilitates them.
