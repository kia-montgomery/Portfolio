The Problem

The organization's onboarding process spanned 14 disconnected systems, required an average of 22 business days to complete, and produced a 31% first-year attrition rate. HR staff spent 8 hours per new hire on manual data entry. New hire NPS was 42 — below the industry average of 58. The cost of doing nothing was estimated at $2.5M+ per year in ongoing attrition and inefficiency.

"The platform will leverage natural language processing to auto-populate forms, machine learning to surface role-specific learning paths, and predictive analytics to flag at-risk new hires within the first 90 days." — Project Charter, Section 1

Scope Decisions

✓ In Scope
  • Vendor evaluation, platform selection, and contract execution
  • Integration with Workday HRIS, ADP Payroll, and ServiceNow IT provisioning
  • AI/ML model configuration for document automation and learning path generation
  • Pilot deployment with two business units (~200 employees)
  • Change management, training, and user adoption programs
  • Full enterprise rollout across all U.S. locations
✗ Out of Scope
  • International offices (deferred to Phase 2)
  • Offboarding or exit workflow automation
  • Compensation and benefits platform redesign
Note: ServiceNow integration was originally out of scope. CR-001 was raised at the kickoff meeting and approved Feb 5 — demonstrating proactive scope management rather than late discovery.
