What This Artifact Demonstrates

Risk management is where PMs earn their keep. This register demonstrates my ability to proactively identify, categorize, and respond to risks across the full spectrum of a complex AI technology implementation — including risks that are often overlooked, like AI model bias and privacy law compliance at the state level.

Each risk entry includes probability, impact, a composite rating, a named owner, a PMI-aligned response strategy (Mitigate, Transfer, Avoid, or Accept), and specific mitigation actions. This isn't a checklist — it's a live management tool.

Risk Response Strategies Breakdown

Mitigate (7 risks)

Reduce the probability or impact of the risk. Used for high-impact risks where we can take direct action — e.g., conducting integration spikes early, launching change management before resistance builds, running bias audits before pilot.

Transfer (1 risk)

Shift the risk to a third party through contract terms. Used for vendor delivery risk (R-003): SLA penalty clauses transfer the financial consequence of late delivery to the vendor.

Avoid (1 risk)

Change the plan to eliminate the risk entirely. For R-005 (UAT scheduling conflict), the risk is avoided by scheduling UAT in June–July — outside the Q4 open enrollment window.

Accept (1 risk)

Acknowledge the risk without active mitigation. Used for R-008 (exec sponsor turnover) — a low-probability risk where documentation and secondary briefings provide passive coverage without overinvesting in prevention.
