What This Artifact Demonstrates

Financial management is a top-tier PM competency — and one that separates program managers from project coordinators. This final artifact presents three financial tracking documents drawn directly from the PM_Portfolio_Financials.xlsx workbook: a category-level budget tracker with quarterly actuals, a 7-person labor and resource utilization report with weekly hours detail, and a contractor cost report with invoice tracking.

Together, these reports demonstrate my ability to own a $1.25M program budget end-to-end — from initial allocation to forecasted completion — with the granularity to answer questions at the category, resource, and invoice level.
